About

Why BridgeStep exists

In many places, students lean on costly private tutoring to make up for gaps in school resources — a solution that isn't available to everyone. BridgeStep offers something different: free, consistent, near-peer mentorship, open to any student who needs it.

Mission: Connect students with near-peer mentors for consistent, goal-driven conversations that build confidence, skills, and a real path forward.

Access Without Cost

Free, always — no exceptions.

Near-Peer Trust

Mentors close in age build real relatability.

Safety First

Every relationship follows a clear Code of Conduct.

Consistency

Regular weekly contact over one-off sessions.
